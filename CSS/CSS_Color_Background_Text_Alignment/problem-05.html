Welcome to the Festival

Experience delicious food, traditional recipes and cultural performances from different regions of India. The Aurin Food & Culture Festival 2026 brings together amazing flavours, talented chefs, traditional artists and food lovers for three exciting days of taste, culture and celebration. Visitors can explore different food zones, enjoy live cooking shows, participate in cultural activities and experience the rich diversity of Indian cuisine.
