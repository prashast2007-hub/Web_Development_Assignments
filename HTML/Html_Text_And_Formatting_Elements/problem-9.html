Student Terms & Rules

Please read all the rules carefully and follow them throughout the academic session.

General Rules

All students must carry their college ID card while entering the campus.
Students without a valid ID card may not be allowed to enter certain academic areas.

Students must maintain proper behavior and respect teachers, staff members, and fellow students. A respectful learning environment is important for everyone.

Attendance

Every student must maintain a minimum of 75% attendance in each subject.

Students having attendance below the required percentage may face detention from examinations or other academic penalties.

Students are advised to attend all lectures regularly to avoid academic difficulties.

Examination

Students must report to the examination hall at least 15 minutes before the examination begins.

The use of mobile phones, smart watches, or any unauthorized material is strictly prohibited.
Any student caught cheating may face cancellation of the examination.

Assignment Submission

All assignments must be submitted on or before the given deadline.

Example: The Mathematics assignment must be submitted by 15 September 2026, before 11:59 PM.

Assignments should be submitted through the official learning portal. Late submissions may receive reduced marks according to the course policy.

Discipline

Students must not engage in bullying, harassment, violence, or any form of misconduct.

Any violation of disciplinary rules may result in a warning, suspension, or other disciplinary action.

Follow the rules. Respect others. Build a better academic environment.

Issued by the Academic Administration
