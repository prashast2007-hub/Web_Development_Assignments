SCIENCE NOTES

A Quick Reference Sheet for Chemistry, Physics and Biology

Chemistry

Chemistry is the branch of science that studies matter, its composition, properties, and chemical changes. Chemical formulas use subscripts to show the number of atoms of each element present in a compound.

A chemical formula represents the elements present in a substance and the number of atoms of each element.

Important Chemical Formulas

  • Water: H2O

  • Carbon Dioxide: CO2

  • Oxygen: O2

  • Sulphuric Acid: H2SO4

  • Glucose: C6H12O6

The subscript numbers in chemical formulas are important because they indicate the exact number of atoms present in a molecule.

Physics

Physics is the study of matter, energy, motion, force, and the laws that govern the natural world. Mathematical expressions are commonly used to represent physical quantities and scientific laws.

Scientific notation and mathematical powers help scientists express very large, very small, and squared or cubed quantities clearly.

Important Scientific Expressions

  • Area:
    Area = length × breadth
    Unit = m2

  • Volume:
    Volume = length × breadth × height
    Unit = m3

  • Speed of Light:
    c = 3 × 108 m/s

  • Newton's Second Law:
    F = ma

  • Kinetic Energy:
    KE = ½mv2

Powers such as m2, m3, and 108 are written using the <sup> tag to correctly display superscript values.

Biology

Biology is the scientific study of living organisms and life processes. It helps us understand how organisms grow, reproduce, obtain energy, and interact with their environment.

The cell is considered the basic structural and functional unit of life.

Important Biology Concepts

  • Cell: The basic unit responsible for the structure and functioning of living organisms.

  • Photosynthesis: The process through which green plants use sunlight to prepare food from carbon dioxide and water.

  • Respiration: The process through which organisms release energy from food.

  • DNA: A molecule that carries important genetic information in living organisms.

Cells, DNA, photosynthesis, and respiration are some of the fundamental concepts used to understand living organisms.

Quick Revision

  • Water: H2O

  • Carbon Dioxide: CO2

  • Area Unit: m2

  • Volume Unit: m3

  • Basic Unit of Life: Cell

  • Important Scientific Concept: Energy is essential for all life processes.
