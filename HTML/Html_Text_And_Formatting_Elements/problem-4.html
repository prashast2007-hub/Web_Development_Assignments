Important Formulas of Algebra, Geometry and Mensuration

Algebra

  • Square of Sum:
    (a + b)2 = a2 + 2ab + b2

  • Square of Difference:
    (a - b)2 = a2 - 2ab + b2

  • Difference of Squares:
    a2 - b2 = (a + b)(a - b)

  • Sum of Squares:
    a2 + b2

  • Quadratic Formula:
    x = [-b ± √(b2 - 4ac)] / 2a

Geometry

  • Area of a Triangle:
    Area = ½ × b × h

  • Pythagoras Theorem:
    c2 = a2 + b2

  • Distance Formula:
    d = √[(x2 - x1)2 + (y2 - y1)2]

  • Area of a Circle:
    A = πr2

  • Circumference of a Circle:
    C = 2πr

Mensuration

  • Volume of a Cube:
    V = a3

  • Total Surface Area of a Cube:
    TSA = 6a2

  • Volume of a Cylinder:
    V = πr2h

  • Volume of a Sphere:
    V = (4/3)πr3

  • Curved Surface Area of a Cylinder:
    CSA = 2πrh

Important Mathematical Notation

  • Superscript: Used for powers such as x2, a2, and r3.

  • Subscript: Used for lower-positioned values such as x1, x2, y1, and y2.
