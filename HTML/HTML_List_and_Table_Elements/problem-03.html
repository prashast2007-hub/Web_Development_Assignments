Introduction

The Department of Computer Science (AIML) provides students with knowledge and practical skills in programming, databases, web development, computer networks and software development.

Important Academic Notice: Students must regularly attend classes and complete all assignments and practical work within the given deadlines.

Faculty Message: We encourage every student to attend classes regularly, participate actively in practical sessions and make consistent progress throughout the semester.

Academic Rules

Students must follow these classroom rules:

  • Students must maintain regular attendance.
  • Students must carry their identity card.
  • Students should arrive in class on time.
  • Students must maintain discipline in classrooms and laboratories.
  • Mobile phones should be kept on silent mode during lectures.
  • Students must submit assignments before the deadline.
  • Students must follow all laboratory safety instructions.

Mandatory Rule: Students must maintain the minimum attendance requirement and complete all mandatory practical sessions.

Practical Class Procedure

Students should follow these steps before attending a practical class:

  1. Check the practical topic in the timetable.
  2. Read the practical instructions before entering the laboratory.
  3. Bring the required notebook and study material.
  4. Reach the laboratory before the practical session begins.
  5. Complete the assigned practical task.
  6. Submit the practical work to the faculty member.

Important Academic Terms

Lecture
A teaching session where the faculty explains theoretical concepts.
Lab
A practical session where students perform experiments or computer-based tasks.
Tutorial
A smaller academic session focused on solving problems and clearing doubts.
Internal Assessment
Evaluation conducted during the semester through tests, assignments, presentations or other activities.
Practical
A hands-on academic session where students apply theoretical knowledge.
