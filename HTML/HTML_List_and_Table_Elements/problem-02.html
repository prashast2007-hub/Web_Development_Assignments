Aurin's Café

Welcome to Aurin's Café, a cozy restaurant serving delicious starters, main courses, desserts and refreshing beverages. Our menu combines classic flavors with creative recipes prepared by our chefs.

Starters

  • Paneer Tikka - Grilled cottage cheese with spices and vegetables.
  • Crispy Corn - Crispy fried corn tossed with herbs and spices.
  • Garlic Bread - Toasted bread with garlic butter and herbs.

Main Course

  • Chef's Special Butter Paneer - Creamy paneer cooked in a rich tomato gravy.
  • Veggie Biryani - Fragrant rice cooked with vegetables and aromatic spices.
  • Cheese Pizza - Freshly baked pizza topped with cheese and vegetables.

Desserts

  • Chocolate Brownie - Warm chocolate brownie served with chocolate sauce.
  • Gulab Jamun - Soft sweet dumplings served with sugar syrup.

Beverages

  • Cold Coffee - Chilled coffee blended with milk and ice cream.
  • Fresh Lime Soda - Refreshing lime drink with soda and mint.
  • Mango Smoothie - Thick and creamy smoothie made with fresh mangoes.

Chef's Recommended Dining Experience

Follow this recommended sequence for the best dining experience:

  1. Start with our Crispy Corn starter.
  2. Enjoy the Chef's Special Butter Paneer with Veggie Biryani.
  3. Finish your meal with our Chocolate Brownie.
  4. Refresh yourself with a Fresh Lime Soda.
