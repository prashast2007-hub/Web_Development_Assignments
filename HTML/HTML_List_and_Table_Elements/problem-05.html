Annual College Fest 2026

Welcome to GLA University's Annual College Fest 2026, a three-day celebration of creativity, talent, technology, music and sports. Students from different departments can participate in exciting competitions, workshops and cultural events.

Fest Introduction

Annual College Fest 2026 brings students together to showcase their talents and compete in various academic, cultural and entertainment activities. The fest will take place over three days and will include competitions, performances, workshops and award ceremonies.

Organizer Message: We invite every student to participate, support their friends and make the Annual College Fest 2026 a memorable experience. Participate with enthusiasm, respect your fellow participants and enjoy every event.

Registration Deadline: 15 September 2026

Registration Process

Follow these steps to register for a competition:

  1. Visit the official fest registration page.
  2. Create your participant account.
  3. Select the competition you want to join.
  4. Enter your personal and college information.
  5. Choose your team members if the event is a team event.
  6. Upload any required documents.
  7. Pay the registration fee if applicable.
  8. Submit the registration form.
  9. Save your registration number for future reference.

Registration Deadline: 15 September 2026

Rules

All participants must follow these general fest rules:

  • Participants must carry a valid college identity card.
  • Participants must report to the venue at least 15 minutes before their event.
  • Participants must follow instructions given by event coordinators.
  • Students must maintain discipline throughout the fest.
  • Any form of cheating or unfair practice may result in disqualification.
  • Participants must respect other students, volunteers and organizers.
  • Damage to college property is strictly prohibited.
  • Participants should keep the event venues clean.

Mandatory Rule: Every participant must follow the instructions of the coordinator and judges. Violation of major rules can result in immediate disqualification.

Important Fest Terms

Open Event
An event in which participants from different departments or colleges can participate.
Team Event
A competition in which two or more participants compete together as a team.
Individual Event
A competition in which one participant competes independently.
Registration
The process of officially signing up for a fest competition.
Coordinator
The person responsible for organizing and managing a particular event.
Final Round
The last stage of a competition where selected participants compete for the final positions.
