University Admission Handbook

Welcome to the GLA University Admission Handbook. This page provides important information about eligibility, application steps, documents, fees and admission contacts.

Admission Overview

GLA University welcomes students who wish to pursue undergraduate and postgraduate programs. Students should carefully check the eligibility requirements and complete the application process before the deadline.

Admission Office Message: Please make sure that all required information and documents are submitted correctly before the application deadline.

Eligibility

Applicants must have successfully completed the required qualifying examination for their chosen program. Eligibility requirements may vary depending on the course.

Mandatory Requirement: Applicants must provide valid academic records and meet the minimum eligibility criteria for the selected program.

Application Deadline: 30 June 2026

Application Process

Follow these steps in the correct order:

  1. Visit the official admission website.
  2. Register using your name, email and mobile number.
  3. Fill in the online application form.
  4. Upload the required documents.
  5. Pay the application fee.
  6. Submit the completed application form.
  7. Attend counselling or admission rounds if required.
  8. Complete the final admission formalities.

Required Documents

Keep the following documents ready:

  • Class 10 marksheet
  • Class 12 marksheet
  • Transfer Certificate
  • Passport-size photographs
  • Government-issued identity proof
  • Migration Certificate, if applicable
  • Entrance examination scorecard, if applicable

Important Terms

Eligibility
The conditions a student must satisfy to apply for a course.
Merit List
A list of candidates prepared according to their academic or entrance performance.
Counselling
A process where eligible students receive guidance and may select their preferred course or seat.
Scholarship
Financial assistance provided to eligible students based on academic performance or other criteria.
Registration
The process of creating an admission account and providing basic applicant information.
