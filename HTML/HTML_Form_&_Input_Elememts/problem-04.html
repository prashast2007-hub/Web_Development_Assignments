Leave Categories

Leave Category Description
Casual Leave For personal or short-term planned absences.
Sick Leave For illness, medical appointments or health-related reasons.
Earned Leave For planned vacations or extended personal leave.
Work From Home For working remotely instead of attending the office.
Other For leave requests that do not fall into the above categories.
