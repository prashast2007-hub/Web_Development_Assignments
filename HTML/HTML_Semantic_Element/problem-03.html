About Aurin Care Clinic

Aurin Care Clinic is designed to provide patients with convenient access to multiple healthcare departments. Our clinic brings together medical professionals, support staff and modern facilities in one location.

We believe that clear communication and respectful patient care are important parts of a positive healthcare experience.

General Medicine

The General Medicine department provides routine consultations and general health assessments. Patients can discuss their health concerns with qualified medical professionals.

Service Type: General Consultation

Cardiology

The Cardiology department provides consultations related to heart and cardiovascular health. Further investigations may be recommended when appropriate.

Service Type: Specialist Consultation

Dermatology

Dermatology consultations focus on skin, hair and nail-related concerns. Patients can discuss their concerns with a qualified specialist.

Service Type: Specialist Consultation

Pediatrics

The Pediatrics department provides healthcare consultations for infants, children and adolescents.

Service Type: Pediatric Consultation

How to Request an Appointment

  1. Review the available departments and doctors.
  2. Check the doctor schedule and preferred time.
  3. Complete the appointment request form.
  4. Provide accurate contact information.
  5. Submit the appointment request.
  6. Wait for confirmation from the clinic.

Healthcare Terms

Consultation
A scheduled discussion between a patient and a healthcare professional regarding the patient's stated healthcare concerns.
Follow-up
A subsequent consultation arranged to review progress or continue an existing care plan.
OPD
OPD stands for Outpatient Department, where patients receive healthcare services without being admitted to the hospital.
Appointment
A scheduled time reserved for a patient consultation with a healthcare professional.

Patient Resources & Notes

Important Patient Instructions

  • Carry a valid identification document when visiting.
  • Bring previous medical reports if they are relevant to your consultation.
  • Arrive approximately 10 minutes before your scheduled appointment.
  • Inform the clinic if you need to cancel or reschedule your appointment.
  • Do not share sensitive personal information through unsecured communication channels.
