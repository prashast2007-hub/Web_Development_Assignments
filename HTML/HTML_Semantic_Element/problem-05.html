Event Introduction

The GLA University National Career & Education Fair 2026 is a comprehensive career-focused event created for students, fresh graduates and job seekers. The fair will provide a platform to meet recruiters, education providers, industry professionals and career experts.

Participants can explore opportunities in sectors such as Information Technology, Engineering, Finance, Healthcare, Management, Education and Government Services.

The event will include career discussions, organization booths, skill-development sessions, education guidance, placement opportunities and networking activities.

Event Objectives

  • Connect students and job seekers with organizations.
  • Provide information about internships and placements.
  • Introduce participants to higher education opportunities.
  • Help students understand current industry expectations.
  • Provide scholarship and apprenticeship information.
  • Encourage professional networking and career planning.
"Our goal is to create a platform where every participant can discover at least one meaningful opportunity for the next step in their career journey."
— Organizing Committee, GLA University

Preparation Guide

Preparing before the fair can help participants make better use of their time and interact more confidently with organizations.

Preparation Tips

  • Prepare an updated resume.
  • Carry multiple copies of your resume.
  • Research organizations you want to approach.
  • Prepare a short professional introduction.
  • Dress appropriately for professional interactions.
  • Prepare questions about jobs, internships and courses.
  • Keep digital copies of important documents.
  • Arrive before your preferred sessions begin.

Important Participant Notice

Participants should carry a valid college or government identification document where applicable.

Registration confirmation may be required for entry to specific sessions or organization interactions.

Seats for selected sessions may be limited and will be available according to the event's registration arrangements.

Attendee Journey

  1. Complete online registration.
  2. Receive registration confirmation.
  3. Arrive at GLA University and complete check-in.
  4. Collect the event schedule and participant information.
  5. Visit organization booths.
  6. Attend career and education sessions.
  7. Network with recruiters and professionals.
  8. Follow up with organizations after the fair.

Information & FAQs

Career Fair Terminology

Career Fair
An organized event where students and job seekers interact with employers and career-related organizations.
Internship
A temporary professional learning opportunity that allows students or graduates to gain practical experience.
Placement
A recruitment opportunity through which an eligible candidate may be considered for employment.
Scholarship
Financial assistance provided to eligible students to support their education.
Apprenticeship
A structured opportunity combining practical workplace experience with learning and training.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who can attend the career fair?

Students, graduates, job seekers and eligible education-focused participants can register for the event.

Should I bring a resume?

Yes. Participants interested in employment or internships are encouraged to bring an updated resume.

Can I attend multiple sessions?

Yes. Participants may attend multiple sessions according to the published schedule and availability.

Is registration required?

Participants should complete the registration process before attending the event.

What should I wear?

Participants seeking employment opportunities are encouraged to wear suitable professional or business-casual clothing.
