Welcome to GLA University Greater Noida

GLA University Greater Noida is an industry-integrated learning campus located at 15A, Knowledge Park II, Greater Noida, Uttar Pradesh.

The Greater Noida campus focuses on practical learning, industry exposure, innovation and career development. Students can explore undergraduate and postgraduate programs in technology, management and computer applications.

The university's current Greater Noida offerings include programs such as B.Tech. CSE, B.Tech. CSE-AIML, B.Tech. Electronics & Computer Engineering, AI-Enabled BBA, AI-Enabled MBA (Hons.) and MCA-AIML.

Academic Programs

GLA University Greater Noida provides programs designed to combine academic knowledge with practical and industry-oriented learning.

B.Tech. Computer Science & Engineering

The B.Tech. CSE program develops strong foundations in programming, computer science, software development, databases and modern technologies.

B.Tech. CSE - Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning

This program focuses on computer science along with Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning, preparing students for emerging technology careers.

B.Tech. Electronics & Computer Engineering

This program combines electronics and computing, with special emphasis on areas such as VLSI and modern computing technologies.

AI-Enabled BBA

The AI-enabled BBA program combines business education with modern technology and analytical skills.

AI-Enabled MBA (Hons.)

This postgraduate management program integrates management concepts with emerging artificial intelligence applications.

MCA - Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning

The MCA-AIML program is designed for students who want advanced knowledge of computer applications, artificial intelligence and machine learning.

Admissions 2026

Important Admission Notice

Admissions are open for the 2026 batch. Prospective students should review their selected program, eligibility requirements and admission procedure before completing the application.

The current admission information lists a process that includes online registration, payment of the registration fee, GLA Entrance Test (GLAET), result checking, interview where applicable, token payment and document submission.

Application Process

  1. Register online with your personal information.
  2. Pay the applicable registration fee.
  3. Appear for the GLA Entrance Test (GLAET), where applicable.
  4. Check the entrance test result.
  5. Attend the interview if shortlisted.
  6. Pay the applicable token amount to secure the seat.
  7. Upload the required academic and identification documents.

For the latest admission procedure, visit the official GLA Greater Noida Admissions page .
