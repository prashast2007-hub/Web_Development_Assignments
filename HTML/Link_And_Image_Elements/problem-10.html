1. About Institution

GLA University is a private university located in Mathura, Uttar Pradesh. The university campus is situated on the Delhi-Mathura Road at P.O. Chaumuhan. GLA University provides undergraduate, postgraduate, doctoral, diploma and certificate programmes across several academic areas.

The institution focuses on academic learning, research, innovation, professional development and extracurricular activities. Its academic environment is designed to provide students with opportunities to develop both technical knowledge and practical skills.

GLA University's academic offerings include areas such as Engineering and Technology, Management, Pharmacy, Computer Applications, Education, Law, Agriculture and Humanities.

The university also promotes research and innovation through research centres, publications, patents and student projects. Its public information states that the institution has 14 research centres and a significant number of research publications and patents.

Students are encouraged to combine classroom learning with practical experience, projects and extracurricular activities.

2. GLA University Campus

The GLA University campus provides an environment where students can attend lectures, participate in practical activities, conduct research and take part in cultural and technical events. The campus is located in Mathura and serves as the main academic centre of the university.

3. Principal / Director Message

Message from the Vice-Chancellor

Prof. Anup Kumar Gupta is the Vice-Chancellor of GLA University. He has been associated with GLA since its inception and has contributed to the development of the university and the introduction of academic programmes.

His academic background includes an M.A., M.Phil. and Ph.D. in English, along with an MBA and an Executive MBA from IIT Bombay. His professional interests include education, administration, institutional development and academic growth.

The university leadership emphasizes quality education, innovation, critical thinking and holistic development as important parts of a student's educational journey.

Students are encouraged to develop knowledge, communication skills, professional abilities and a sense of responsibility towards society.

4. Academic Programs

GLA University offers a broad range of academic programmes. Students can choose programmes according to their interests, career objectives and academic qualifications.

Engineering and Technology

Engineering programmes include areas such as Computer Science and Engineering, Electronics and Communication Engineering and Electrical Engineering. Students work on technical concepts, laboratory activities, programming, engineering design and practical projects.

Computer Applications

Computer-focused programmes help students develop knowledge of programming, databases, software development, data science and modern computing technologies.

Management

Management programmes introduce students to business, entrepreneurship, finance, marketing, human resources and organizational behaviour.

Pharmacy

Pharmacy programmes provide students with knowledge related to medicines, pharmaceutical sciences, laboratories and healthcare-related applications.

Higher Research

GLA University also offers doctoral programmes in several disciplines. Research students work towards contributing new knowledge through systematic academic investigation.

Visit the official academic programmes page for current programme information.

5. Academic Activities

Academic life at GLA University extends beyond traditional classroom teaching. Students participate in technical projects, workshops, laboratory activities, research initiatives, seminars and industry-oriented learning experiences.

These activities provide opportunities to apply theoretical knowledge to practical situations and develop communication, teamwork and problem-solving skills.

6. Campus Facilities

GLA University provides facilities intended to support academic learning, research and student development. Students have access to classrooms, laboratories, library resources, computing facilities, hostels, sports areas and other campus services.

Laboratories and Research Facilities

Laboratories provide students with opportunities to perform practical experiments and understand how concepts taught in classrooms are applied in real-world situations.

Library

Academic library resources support students in preparing for examinations, completing assignments, conducting research and developing independent learning habits.

Sports and Recreation

Sports and recreational activities provide students with opportunities to maintain physical fitness while developing teamwork, discipline and leadership skills.

7. Student Life

Student life at GLA University includes academic activities, cultural programmes, technical events, sports competitions, clubs, workshops and other extracurricular opportunities.

Students can participate in activities that allow them to discover interests outside their academic programmes. These experiences can help develop leadership, teamwork, communication and organizational skills.

8. Admission Information

GLA University admissions provide students with opportunities to apply for undergraduate, postgraduate, doctoral, diploma and certificate programmes. Eligibility requirements depend on the programme selected by the applicant.

For many programmes, admission involves the GLA Entrance Test (GLAET). Applicants should carefully check the eligibility criteria, application process, examination information and required documents before applying.

Always verify the latest admission dates and eligibility requirements on the official GLA University website before submitting an application.

The university's admission information states that applicants can register online and proceed through the admission process using the official admission portal.
