3. Cloud Computing and the Modern Internet

Cloud computing has transformed the way individuals and companies store and access information. Instead of keeping everything on a single computer, users can access applications and files through internet-based services.

Cloud platforms are used for websites, applications, databases, online storage, and many other services. They allow businesses to scale their systems without purchasing large amounts of physical hardware.

Cloud computing has become a major part of modern technology infrastructure. As internet connectivity improves, cloud-based services will continue to grow. Learning cloud technology can be valuable for future developers.
