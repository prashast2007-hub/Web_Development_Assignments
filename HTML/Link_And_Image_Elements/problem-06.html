Introduction

GLA University proudly presents TechFest 2026, an exciting technology festival designed to bring together students, innovators, developers, researchers and technology enthusiasts from across the country. The event will provide a platform for participants to explore modern technologies, share creative ideas and develop solutions for real-world challenges.

From artificial intelligence and robotics to web development, cybersecurity and sustainable technology, TechFest 2026 will offer an energetic environment filled with learning, competition and innovation. Whether you are an experienced programmer or simply curious about the future of technology, there will be something exciting waiting for you.

TechFest 2026 will feature a wide range of exciting activities where students can test their skills, collaborate with others and learn from industry professionals. Participants will get the opportunity to work on innovative projects and compete in challenging technical events.
